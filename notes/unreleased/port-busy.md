---
title: A taken port says who is on it
scope: server
---

Start the office twice and the second one died with a stack trace ending in `EADDRINUSE 127.0.0.1:5177`. The person who ran it knew what that meant only if they had read Node before; the person who had just pasted the one-liner from valey.dev did not, and the message did not say the one useful thing — that their office was already running one tab away.

Now a taken port is asked who it is. Another office there means this one prints the address and the version of the running one and exits cleanly: two `npm start` in a row give one office, not an error. Something that is not Valey on the port means the office moves one port up and says so, together with the line that already explains what a non-canonical port costs — Claude Code sends its questions to 5177. Nothing on the port is ever stopped: the office asks, and leaves whatever answers alone. The office wears a name tag for this, `/api/version`, readable without an owner token — it says nothing the page did not already show.
