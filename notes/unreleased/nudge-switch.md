---
title: The video reminder can be put away
scope: office
---

After every minor release the entrance showed a card asking for the release video — useful while videos are part of the work, noise while they are not.

Now `"releaseNudge": false` in `~/.config/valey/settings.json` puts the card away. Nothing else changes: a draft of the video script is still written at every minor, so turning the card back on finds the drafts waiting. Deliberately left out: a switch in the office itself — this is a setting one flips rarely, and it lives with the others.
