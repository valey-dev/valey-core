---
title: A feature note shows the feature or says why it cannot
scope: release
nopicture: the change is a refusal printed by the release script in the terminal
---

Thirteen of the last seventeen feature notes went out as bare text, six of them for things that are plainly on the screen. Nothing asked for a picture: the template came with a shot, and deleting it was the short way.

Now a note carries a picture or says why it has none, and the release refuses one with neither. A recipe can also prepare the office before the camera — put a request in the pager, flip a switch — which is how the pager and the newsstand got their pictures in this release, taken against their own versions.
