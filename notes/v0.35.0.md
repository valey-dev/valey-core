# v0.35.0 — 11 September 2026

## A shipped release of the Modules reaches the buyers' shelf in the same tail

*release*

The Modules had their own way to reach a buyer — `tools/publish.mjs` cuts the archive from a tag and puts it on the buyers' repository with a release page — and it was a separate command nobody ran. On 11 September the shelf stood at v0.6.2 while the Modules were at v0.7.1: two releases, with the fixes that made the phone's feed and the meeting-room voice work on the current office, had never reached the archive a buyer downloads. A buyer's install of that archive started cleanly and said nothing — the breakage was in behaviour, not in the log.

Now the release tail runs the repository's own `publish.mjs` when there is one, after the pages and before the public remote. The office has no such file and skips the step; the Modules have it, and every shipped release lands on the shelf. As with the public remote, nothing new is published by the script: the merge that made the release is the owner's decision, the tail carries it.
